# Listicle ("N Things/Tools/Tips") Article Template

## Structure
1. **Hook** — Why this list, why now (pain point, trend, common request)
2. **Items** — Each with subheading, explanation, concrete example
3. **Honorable mentions** — Quick shout-outs that didn't make the main list
4. **How to choose** — Decision criteria for picking from the list

## Tone
- Punchy, scannable, "here's what you need to know"
- Each item stands alone — readers will skip around
- Lead each item with the takeaway, then support it

## Code Density: Medium (15-30%)

## Target Length: 1500-3000 words

## Reader Promise
"After reading this, you'll have a curated shortlist"
