# Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Article Template

## Structure
1. **Context** — What prompted this decision (problem, constraint, opportunity)
2. **Decision** — What was decided, stated clearly and concisely
3. **Status** — Proposed, accepted, deprecated, or superseded
4. **Consequences** — Trade-offs accepted (positive and negative)
5. **Alternatives considered** — Other options evaluated and why they were rejected
6. **Decision criteria** — The factors that drove the final choice

## Tone
- Neutral, analytical, future-reader-oriented
- Write for someone reading this 2 years from now who asks "why?"
- Present facts and reasoning, not advocacy

## Code Density: Medium (15-30%)

## Target Length: 1000-2000 words

## Reader Promise
"After reading this, you'll understand WHY this decision was made"
