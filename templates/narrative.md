# Narrative / War Story Article Template

## Structure
1. **Hook** — The moment everything changed / went wrong
2. **Setup** — The situation, the team, the constraints
3. **Crisis** — What happened, why it was hard
4. **Journey** — What you tried, what failed, what you learned
5. **Key Insight** — The breakthrough moment
6. **Resolution** — How it turned out
7. **Takeaway** — The lesson others can apply

## Tone
- Personal, engaging, "let me tell you what happened"
- Strong narrative arc — tension, discovery, resolution
- Vulnerability — admit mistakes, share the messy middle

## Code Density: Low-Medium (15-25%)

## Target Length: 1500-3000 words

## Reader Promise
"After reading this, you'll think differently about X"
